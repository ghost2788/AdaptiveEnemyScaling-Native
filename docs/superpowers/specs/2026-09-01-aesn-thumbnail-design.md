# AESN Twin Threat Thumbnail Design

## Purpose

Replace the existing balance-scale thumbnail with a stronger visual identity centered on AESN's empowered enemies. The thumbnail must communicate the mod's two enemy-enhancement pillars at a glance without relying on lettering.

## Approved Composition

Use a square, text-free **Twin Threat Crest** composition built from the approved AESN status-emblem families:

- Place the gold Hardened guardian in the lower-left half.
- Place the icy-cyan Relentless face in the upper-right half.
- Give the two emblems equal visual prominence and overlap them slightly near the center so they read as one crest.
- Preserve both emblems' dark internal infill and luminous linework.
- Use a deep near-black burgundy/navy background.
- Surround the combined crest with a restrained BG3-style circular gold frame.

Do not include scales, stat bars, progression arrows, lettering, particles, extra symbols, scenery, or additional characters.

## Visual Priorities

1. Both enemy emblems remain recognizable at small thumbnail sizes.
2. Gold-versus-cyan contrast separates Hardened from Relentless.
3. The combined silhouette feels dangerous, premium, and consistent with BG3 condition-icon presentation.
4. The circular frame supports the composition without competing with the two faces.

## Deliverables

- One full-resolution square PNG master with an opaque finished background.
- One project-ready 1254×1254 `thumbnail.png` replacement, matching the existing asset dimensions.
- A comparison preview showing the result at full size, 256 px, 128 px, and 64 px.
- Preserve the previous thumbnail as a recoverable source asset rather than deleting it.

## Acceptance Criteria

- No text appears at any size.
- Hardened and Relentless have similar apparent size; neither occupies more than 60% of the combined crest area.
- Neither emblem is cropped through its defining face or armor silhouette.
- The 64 px preview still reads as two distinct empowered-enemy emblems.
- No transparency artifacts, checkerboard pixels, white fringe, or unintended background cutouts are present.
